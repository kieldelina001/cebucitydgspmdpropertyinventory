Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cebu City DGS PMD Property Inventory web application handles information when you use the system.

1. Purpose of the Application

The Cebu City DGS PMD Property Inventory is an internal property management and inventory system used to manage and monitor property information under the Property Management Division.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using your Google account, the application may receive basic account information such as your Google account email address.

The application uses this information primarily to verify whether the user is authorized to access the system.

3. Google Account Information

The application may use Google authentication services to identify authorized users. We do not use your Google account information for advertising or commercial purposes.

4. Google Sheets

The application may access information stored in authorized Google Sheets used by the Property Management Division.

Access to these spreadsheets is limited to authorized users and is subject to the permissions configured by the system administrator.

5. Use of Information

Information accessed by the application may be used for:

  • User authentication and authorization
  • Property inventory management
  • Recording and updating property information
  • Maintaining system security
  • Administrative and operational purposes

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or commercially distribute personal information obtained through the application.

Information may only be accessed by authorized personnel as necessary for official property management and administrative purposes.

7. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information handled by the application. Access to the system is restricted to authorized users.

8. Data Retention

Property and inventory information may be retained for official administrative and record-keeping purposes in accordance with applicable policies and requirements.

9. Google Services

This application uses Google services for authentication and/or data storage. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or access to the application, please contact the administrator or the Property Management Division.
