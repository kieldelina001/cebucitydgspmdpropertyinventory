Cebu City DGS PMD Property Inventory

Property Management Division

Property Inventory Management System

The Cebu City DGS PMD Property Inventory is a web-based property management system developed to support the Property Management Division in maintaining and monitoring property inventory information.

Authorized Users Only
Access to the property inventory system is restricted to authorized personnel. Google Sign-In is used to identify and authenticate authorized users.

About the Application

The system provides authorized personnel with tools for managing, monitoring, and reviewing property inventory records.

Information may be stored and managed using authorized Google services, including Google Sheets, for official property management and administrative purposes.

Google Sign-In

This application uses Google authentication to identify users and determine whether their Google account has been authorized to access the system.

The application may receive basic Google account information, such as the user's email address, for authentication and access-control purposes.

Google account information is not used for advertising or commercial purposes.

Access to Property Information

Property information is intended for authorized administrative and property management activities. Access to the application's information is controlled through user authorization and Google account permissions.
